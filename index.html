Build the arterial tree

Start at the arch of the aorta and work out to the smallest branches. Drag each term from the word bank onto its box in the tree, or tap a term and then tap a box. When a branch is right, blood flows into it.

How do you want to play?

Your score is your time plus 10 seconds for each mistake and 20 seconds for each hint. Lower is better.

Leaderboard

Score is time plus 10 seconds per mistake and 20 seconds per hint. Lower is better. Each player's best run is shown; select a name to see all of their runs.

About

Arterial Blood Supply of the Head and Neck

A study game: build the arterial tree of the head and neck, from the arch of the aorta out to the last branch of the maxillary artery.
